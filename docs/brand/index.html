Name

The Chinese and the English say the same thing

Chinese 画说
话说→画说

“话说天下大势……” (“the story goes: the great affairs of the realm…”) is how a traditional storyteller opens. Swap 话 (speech) for 画 (picture) and you have what this product does: pictures that speak.

English Frametell
frame=画·tell=说

A frame is a viewfinder, and also one frame of a picture; to tell is to explain. Frame a picture and tell it clearly. The name and the mark say the same thing: 「」 is that frame. In English, a frame story is also a way of telling a story.

Write “画说” in Chinese contexts and “Frametell” in English ones; when both appear, Chinese comes first. The endorsement by Sitor Team appears only in the full lockup, the closing card and the README.

Mark

The quotation marks are “tell”, the viewfinder is “frame”

  1. Two corners. 「 top left and 」 bottom right: both Chinese quotation marks and the two corners of a viewfinder. Stroke 6.5, arm length 23, all within a 48 × 48 grid.
  2. One dot. The caramel dot in the middle is the recording light and the focus of the picture. Keep a ring of clear space around it (dashed) so it never touches the corners.
  3. Only two colours. Corners in ink or paper, the dot in caramel. No gradients, outlines or drop shadows.

Usage

A few hard lines

Do

  • Leave clear space round the mark of at least one corner's stroke width (about 1/8 of the mark's width).
  • The corners are always top left and bottom right, matching the direction of Chinese quotation marks.
  • At 24px and below, switch to the small-size version.
  • Quotations, framing and focus cues in the picture may borrow the language of the 「」 corners.

Don't

  • Don't complete it to four corners: four corners make a QR-code scanner frame and lose the quotation-mark meaning.
  • Don't turn the dot into a triangular play button or change its colour.
  • No gradients, glows, outlines or drop shadows.
  • The English name is always written Frametell: only the first letter capitalised, never FRAMETELL in all capitals anywhere. Never “画说 Studio” or “Frametell AI”; “by Sitor Team” never goes in the interface's top bar or the video corner bug.
