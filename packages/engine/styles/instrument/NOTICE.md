The grammar card, the kit and the background are original work for Frametell Cloud, except for these techniques adapted
in `kit.js`: the camera at any azimuth and elevation, the prism extruded from a plan with its sides shaded by facing and
its silhouette and crease lines, rounded plans, knurl lines, coil springs split into back and front halves, and ruler and
radial ticks. They come from Anatomy (https://github.com/wheresryan22/anatomy), rewritten for a 2D canvas and film time.
The figure card's corner layout (figure number, title, legend, live readout) and the tone hierarchy (one lit part) follow
its design.
