Parts of `kit.js` (the carving method — dye sprites cut with destination-out, a tileable hide texture multiplied in
with the alpha restored, the crescent / lattice / coin / cloud-scroll / meander / strata cut patterns, hooked flame
tongues, thread rivets — the lamp-light model and the press-on / lift-off motion) are adapted from the shadow-puppet
style of lemo-opuscar, https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar (re-implemented for this engine; no puppet, scene or
story of theirs is copied).
