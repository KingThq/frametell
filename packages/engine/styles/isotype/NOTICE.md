The Isotype pack (style.js, style.css, kit.js, GRAMMAR.md) is original work written for Frametell Cloud. It follows the
conventions of the Vienna Method of pictorial statistics (Otto Neurath, Marie Neurath and Gerd Arntz, 1920s–1930s) as a
tradition; no pictogram, chart or artwork from the Isotype archive is copied. Every pictogram in kit.js was drawn from
scratch out of circles, rectangles and simple polygons, and the counting, cutting and migration logic was written from
scratch; no third-party source was adapted.

Fonts are loaded from the shared `_fonts` directory: Jost and Noto Sans SC (SIL Open Font License 1.1) and
IBM Plex Mono (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
