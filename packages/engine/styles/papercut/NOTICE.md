# Notice

`kit.js` re-implements, for this engine, techniques from the **papercut-red** style of **Lemo-Opuscar**
(`styles/papercut-red/demo/paper.js`, `motifs.js`, `tuanhua.js`, `light.js`, `fold.js`, and `STYLE.md`): the neutral paper
grain laid in soft-light, the light/dark cut-edge rim, the folk cut vocabulary (sawtooth rows, crescents, tapered slits,
swirls, plum and coin holes, the inset double contour), the rosette drawn as one wedge mirrored round the centre, backlit
transmission and light rays from the hole mask, and the fold flap that squashes across a mirror line.
