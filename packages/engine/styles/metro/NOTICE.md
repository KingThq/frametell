The Transit Map pack (style.js, style.css, kit.js, GRAMMAR.md) is original work written for Frametell Cloud. It follows
the visual conventions of the schematic metro diagram (Harry Beck's 1933 London Underground diagram and its successors)
as a tradition; no code, artwork, typeface or trademark from any transit authority is used. The octilinear router,
rounded bends, label placement and train timing in kit.js were written from scratch; no third-party source was adapted.

Fonts are loaded from the shared `_fonts` directory: Inter Tight and Noto Sans SC (SIL Open Font License 1.1) and
IBM Plex Mono (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
