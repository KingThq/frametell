The kit's projection, three-band shading, thickness-weighted silhouette ink, cast shadow, board and part labels are
adapted from Colorfigures by April Zhu (MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 April Zhu), which is built on iso-glow (April Zhu,
MIT) and iso-figure by Tolga Cohce (MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 Tolga Cohce). The original draws SVG in a browser
page with buttons; this kit re-writes those ideas for the engine's canvas and page-local time. The materials' colours
follow Colorfigures' palettes. The wobble, the step pills, the readings, the grammar card and the rest of the kit are
original work for Frametell Cloud.
