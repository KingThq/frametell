Parts of `kit.js` (the off-register colour cel and its jitter on twos, the pressure ink ribbon with breaks, the dry-brush
tooth, the two-plate lettering, the curved manual arrow, and the ornaments: sparkle, starburst, rays, boomerang and
kidney) are adapted from the midcentury-toon style of Lemo-Opuscar, https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar (styles/midcentury-toon),
and re-implemented for this engine.
