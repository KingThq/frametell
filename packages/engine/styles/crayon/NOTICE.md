# Notice

The crayon kit (`kit.js`), grammar card, paper and page turn are written for Frametell Cloud. Several techniques are
adapted (re-implemented, not copied) from **crayon-book** in LemoLab's style library, MIT licence, Copyright (c) 2026
LemoLab: wax adhering only to the peaks of the paper's tooth as a pressure threshold, crayon strokes as ribbons with
tapered ends, a denser core and streaks, zigzag hatching that overshoots the outline, boiling lines on twos, and the
knock-out that keeps paper behind a figure.
